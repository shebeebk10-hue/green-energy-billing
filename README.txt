GREEN ENERGY BILLING SYSTEM

HOW TO USE
1. Extract the ZIP file to a folder on your Windows computer.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser; no installation, server, account, or database is needed.
4. Enter invoice details and customer name/phone.
5. Fill in an item, quantity, and price. Use + Add item for more rows. One item works perfectly too.
6. Click Download Bill PDF. In the print dialog, select “Save as PDF” as the destination and click Save.

NOTES
- PDFs are generated through your browser's print-to-PDF function. This app does not upload or store PDF files.
- Keep the images folder beside index.html; the original supplied images are used directly so their artwork and text are not retyped or recreated.
- Internet is not required for the app itself.
